parser: the shared parser reads the day as the month

Every tenant on file writes the day first. The shared parser read every date month first, so “7 April” came out as 4 July. This is the bug the #4471 page describes. It covers reports from the app, from email and from the booking-v2 form, at every tenant. The desk counts the one-day reports here too, where the tenant rule files them. Fixed on 5 October by “fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales”, the change that closed #4471.

hint: booking-v3 tells people to type the month first

The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. At a day-first tenant, people type what the hint says, month first, and the form reads it the tenant’s way, day first. The date is already flipped before the parser sees it, so the parser fix does not reach these reports. Engineering won’t fix it: the booking-v3 form is retired, so no new reports come in this way.

one-day: the date moves by one day

A flipped day and month moves a date by weeks or months, never by a day. The desk read these reports as a one-day move (desk-notes.json). The tenant rule files them under parser, and the desk counts them there, but they describe something else: the same meeting on two days in two cities, a date shown as the day before, an invite older than its booking, one month that is always a day off. The parser fix does not explain them, and nothing else has shipped for them. The cause is not known yet. Time zones and the day boundary are the first thing to check, then June.
